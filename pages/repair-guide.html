Guide 01

The 80-20 Rule for Lithium Batteries

Keeping your lithium-ion battery between 20% and 80% charge level reduces chemical stress on cathode layers, doubling total cycle lifespan from 500 to 1,000 charge cycles.

Guide 02

GaN vs Traditional Silicon Chargers

Gallium Nitride (GaN) semiconductors generate 40% less heat while conducting higher voltage, allowing ultra-compact 100W chargers that won't overheat your device.
