Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a screen repair take?

Most screen replacements are completed within 1–2 hours while you wait. We'll give you an exact time estimate when you drop off your device.

Do you offer a warranty on repairs?

Yes. All repairs come with a 90-day warranty covering parts and labour. If the same issue returns, we fix it at no extra cost.

Can I trade in my old phone?

Absolutely. Bring any working smartphone and we'll assess it on the spot. The trade-in value is applied directly to your new purchase.

Do you sell unlocked phones?

Yes, all smartphones we stock are unlocked and compatible with any carrier. Our team can help you verify compatibility before you buy.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and buy-now-pay-later options via Klarna.

Can I book a repair appointment online?

Yes — use the form above, select “Repair enquiry”, describe your issue and we'll confirm a slot via email or phone within 2 hours.
